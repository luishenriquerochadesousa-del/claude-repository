# Contract clause checklist — leasing a property to operate as Alojamento Local

Hand this to your lawyer alongside the draft contract. It is a checklist of what
must be present, not a template — the right structure depends on whether you are
signing an *arrendamento*, a *cessão de exploração*, or a management mandate.

---

## 1. Identification and legitimacy

- [ ] Full identification of the owner, and confirmation they are the registered
      owner on the **caderneta predial** and **certidão permanente** (ask for both).
- [ ] If the property is co-owned, **all** co-owners sign.
- [ ] If there is a mortgage, confirm the loan terms do not prohibit letting or
      non-residential use.
- [ ] Confirmation there is no existing RNAL registration on the property — or,
      if there is, agreement on who holds it going forward.

## 2. The authorisation clauses — the ones that matter

- [ ] **Express written authorisation to sublet** (art. 1088.º do Código Civil).
      Without it, subletting breaches art. 1038.º f) and grounds termination of
      your own lease.
- [ ] **Express written authorisation to exploit the property as Alojamento
      Local**, naming the activity. Generic "commercial use" language is not
      enough for the câmara's legitimacy check.
- [ ] **Consent for the RNAL registration to be made in the tenant's name**, with
      the owner undertaking to sign any document the câmara requires.
- [ ] Owner's obligation to communicate the sublease to their own insurer, and to
      not oppose the activity for the duration of the contract.

## 3. Term and exit

- [ ] Minimum term of **3–5 years**, with a renewal option in your favour.
      Anything shorter cannot amortise a €8,000–15,000 fit-out.
- [ ] **Regulatory-change clause**: you may terminate without penalty if the
      município revokes or suspends the registration, declares the area an *área
      de contenção* affecting your registration, or a condominium opposition
      deliberation succeeds.
- [ ] Notice period for ordinary termination by either side, and what happens to
      bookings already confirmed on the calendar.
- [ ] What happens on sale of the property. Since DL 76/2024 the AL registration
      transfers with the property — make sure the lease binds a new owner too.

## 4. Warranties from the owner

- [ ] The **título constitutivo da propriedade horizontal** does not prohibit
      non-residential use of the fraction.
- [ ] No condominium deliberation opposing Alojamento Local exists (attach the
      last two years of assembly minutes as an annex).
- [ ] The property has a valid **licença de utilização** and the *caderneta*
      matches its actual configuration.
- [ ] Habitability: no known structural, damp or infiltration issues.

## 5. Money

- [ ] Rent amount, payment date, and index/review mechanism (cap any annual
      increase).
- [ ] Deposit amount and the exact conditions for its return.
- [ ] Cost split, written explicitly:
      - **Owner**: IMI, condominium quotas, structural repairs, appliance
        replacement at end of life, building insurance.
      - **Tenant/operator**: utilities, internet, consumables, cleaning,
        cosmetic wear, civil liability insurance, the AL registration and its
        obligations.
- [ ] If the structure is a **cessão de exploração** rather than a lease, settle
      the VAT treatment in writing. Following AT binding information PIV 27356
      (March 2025), what the owner receives from the operator is taxed at the
      standard 23% rate, whereas a pure *arrendamento* is VAT-exempt under
      art. 9.º n.º 29 CIVA. This is a 23% swing on your largest cost line.

## 6. The property itself

- [ ] Detailed inventory and photographic record annexed, signed by both sides.
- [ ] **Ownership of furniture and equipment installed by you is yours**, and may
      be removed at the end of the contract.
- [ ] Right to make non-structural alterations (paint, lighting, smart lock,
      furniture) without prior consent; structural work with consent.
- [ ] Condition in which the property is returned — fair wear and tear excluded.

## 7. Operations and neighbours

- [ ] Owner's right to inspect, with reasonable notice, at agreed intervals
      (quarterly is normal and is usually what closes a hesitant landlord).
- [ ] Your undertaking to enforce house rules, quiet hours and a no-party policy,
      and to maintain the mandatory safety equipment.
- [ ] Your **civil liability insurance of at least €75,000 per claim**, with the
      owner named or evidenced, and proof provided annually.
- [ ] Damage protocol: what you claim, what the owner claims, what the platform's
      guarantee covers, and the excess.
- [ ] Confidentiality/data: guest data handling under RGPD, and SIBA reporting is
      the operator's responsibility.

## 8. Annexes to attach

1. Caderneta predial urbana and certidão permanente
2. Título constitutivo da propriedade horizontal
3. Condominium assembly minutes, last two years
4. Licença de utilização
5. Signed inventory with photographs
6. Copy of the civil liability insurance policy
7. The written AL and subletting authorisation, as a standalone signed document

---

*Not legal advice. Budget €300–600 for a lawyer to review the package — it is the
cheapest insurance in this business.*
