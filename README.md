# Airbnb-Business — Alojamento Local without owning the property

A practical operator's guide to starting a short-term-rental business in Portugal
**without being the owner of the property**, delivered as a single self-contained
HTML dashboard.

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any browser. No build step, no dependencies,
no network calls.

## The short version

1. **Written authorisation or nothing.** Under arts. 1038.º f) and 1088.º of the
   Código Civil a tenant cannot give a third party use of the property; the
   owner's consent to sublet — and specifically to operate Alojamento Local —
   must be in writing. It is also what the câmara checks when you register.
2. **Check the freguesia before you check the flat.** Lisboa's central parishes,
   Porto's historic core and Funchal's Sé are closed to new registrations.
   Municipalities have until 31 December 2026 to rewrite their regulations
   under DL 151/2026, and may keep suspending registrations meanwhile.
3. **Registration is instant, compliance is not.** The RNAL number is issued on
   submission, but the município inspects within 60 days (90 in a containment
   area) and ASAE fines start at €2,500 for an individual.
4. **Rent under 30% of realistic gross, or walk away.** Portuguese rents relative
   to achievable ADR make pure arbitrage thin. Co-hosting at 15–25% of gross
   carries no rent risk and near-zero capital — it is the better entry point.
5. **Two tax items catch everyone**: the 23% reverse charge you must self-assess
   on Airbnb and Booking commissions even while VAT-exempt, and the €15,000
   art. 53.º threshold.

## Status and provenance

Compiled August 2026 from primary legislation (DRE), Turismo de Portugal, ASAE,
Portal das Finanças, TravelBI market data, and law-firm commentary. Every source
is linked in section 13 of the dashboard.

**This is research, not legal or tax advice.** Portuguese AL rules are being
rewritten at municipal level through the end of 2026 and tax thresholds move
annually. Have a lawyer review the contract package and a *contabilista
certificado* confirm the tax position before you sign a lease or file a
comunicação prévia.
